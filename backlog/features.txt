Watch together. A shared “watch party” room is the big one. A smaller version is “continue on another device” with a QR code.
Smart “Because you watched” rows using genre overlap, then upgrade to real recommendations once you have viewing data.
Seasonal and weekly schedule. For airing anime, a “this week” calendar showing which episodes drop on which day, with a notification bell.
Collections and playlists that profiles can create, name and reorder, plus admin-made editorial collections like “Cyberpunk anime” or “Weekend binge”.
Ratings and short reviews. Do thumbs up/down first, then written reviews once you have reporting and moderation.
Download for offline only for content you own and only if your device and licensing rules allow it.
Parental controls. A kids profile with a maturity cap and a PIN to switch profiles or change settings.
Chromecast and AirPlay support on the player.
Smarter autoplay. Remember the audio and subtitle language per profile and apply it to every episode automatically.
Analytics for you as owner. Most-watched titles, where viewers stop watching, failed playback counts and storage used, shown in the admin dashboard.
Content requests. Let viewers request a title, with an admin queue to approve or reject.
Backup and export. One-click export of your catalog and viewing data, which also helps if you move storage providers.
